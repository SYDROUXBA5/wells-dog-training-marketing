Why 100% direct response, £0 on brand

The reasoning behind the plan

What counts as direct response? Spending that prompts an action we can count, such as a search-ad click, a lead form, a voucher code, a market-stall sign-up or a referral. Brand is spending that builds recognition over time with no immediate action attached.

When brand spending starts: in year 2, paid for out of year-1 revenue, once there are clients and reviews to build on. The research recommends a larger brand share for established businesses, not for launches.
